# Questionnaire — Internal Operations Stakeholders

| Status | Owner | Last updated |
| ------ | ----- | ------------ |
| Draft — not yet used | Project owner | 2026-11-24 |

**Purpose:** decide who will run, support, and secure the platform once it carries real traffic.

**Respondents:** the owner and whoever will be responsible for operations, customer support, compliance
review, and infrastructure (internal staff or prospective outsourced providers). If nobody holds a role
yet, the answer "not assigned" is itself important evidence.

| # | Question | Register IDs | P |
| - | -------- | ------------ | - |
| OP-01 | Who will monitor the platform outside office hours, and who is on call when telco connections fail at night? | D-066 | P1 |
| OP-02 | What availability and response times will we promise customers, and can we staff to meet them? | D-067, D-079 | P1 |
| OP-03 | Who handles customer support (hours, channels, first and second line)? | D-079 | P2 |
| OP-04 | Who reviews sender ID requests, campaign approvals, and abuse alerts, and how quickly must they respond? | D-078, D-087 | P1 |
| OP-05 | Who leads an incident (outage, data breach, abuse)? Who notifies customers, telcos, and regulators? | D-077, D-121 | P2 |
| OP-06 | Where should the platform be hosted (cloud region, local data centre, hybrid)? Who owns and pays for the accounts? | D-068, D-044 | P1 |
| OP-07 | Can the chosen hosting meet telco connectivity needs (static IPs, VPN endpoints)? | D-044, D-102 | P1 |
| OP-08 | How much data loss and downtime is acceptable after a major failure? | D-076 | P1 |
| OP-09 | Who has production access, how is it granted and revoked, and how is it reviewed? | D-077 | P2 |
| OP-10 | Who reconciles our usage records with telco invoices each month? | D-108, D-093 | P1 |
| OP-11 | What operating budget is available for monitoring tools, hosting, and staff? | D-090 | P0 |

**Evidence to request:** staffing plan or outsourcing proposals; hosting quotes; written decisions on
service levels and recovery targets.
