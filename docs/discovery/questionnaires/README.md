## How to use them

1. **Before the meeting:** pick the P0 and P1 questions relevant to this stakeholder (marked in each
   questionnaire). Send the list in advance where appropriate.
2. **During:** record who answered and in what capacity. Ask for written confirmation or documents for
   anything important.
3. **After:** log each answer as evidence in the [evidence register](../evidence-register.md) and propose
   register updates for owner review. Answers given verbally are leads until confirmed in writing.

## Rules

- **Do not write confidential answers into these files.** Agreements, rate cards, technical
  specifications, credentials, and customer volumes under NDA belong in the owner's secure document store;
  the evidence register keeps only a reference.
- Do not present internal assumptions to stakeholders as facts (e.g. do not tell a telco we "have" 5
  billion messages a month).
- Do not ask stakeholders questions outside their competence (e.g. legal interpretations from telco sales
  staff, or technical specifications from customers).
