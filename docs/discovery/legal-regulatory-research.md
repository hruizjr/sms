## How to read this log

**Source classification**

| Class | Meaning |
| ----- | ------- |
| **Official primary source** | Published by the issuing body itself (e.g. NTC, NPC, Official Gazette) |
| **Official reproduction** | Government-hosted reproduction of the text (e.g. Supreme Court E-Library, Senate Legislative Reference) |
| **Official register record** | Filing metadata in the Office of the National Administrative Register (ONAR), searched via the UP Law Center's NARO site |
| **Unofficial full-text copy** | Full text reproduced by a private legal library (here lawphil.net and dev.lawyerly.ph). Useful for reading, but must be checked against an official copy before reliance |
| **Secondary legal commentary** | Law-firm alerts, news articles, summaries — **leads only** |
| **Unverified lead** | An issuance reported to exist whose text was not read |

**Access status** — *Read* (text actually read), *Partly read*, *Not accessible from this research
environment* (the page refused automated access), *Metadata only*.

### LR-06 — NTC MC No. 03-03-2005 (Rules and Regulations on Broadcast Messaging Service)

| Field | Detail |
| ----- | ------ |
| Official source URL | https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/10/42711 — Official reproduction, **not accessible from this research environment** (HTTP 403) |
| Copy read | https://dev.lawyerly.ph/laws/42711 — Unofficial full-text copy, Read in full |
| Date of issuance | 15 March 2005 |
| Publication / effectivity | Sec. 7.1: 15 days after publication (publication date not verified) |
| Relevant provisions | **Scope (Sec. 1):** applies to "All Public Telecom Entities and content providers"; covers "commercial and promotional advertisements, surveys sent via Broadcast/Push messaging service" (1.2); excludes emergency, distress, public service, network, and consumer welfare advisories (1.3). **Definitions (Sec. 2):** broadcast messaging service; content provider; opt-in; opt-out; push messages (sent "without a user request and are initiated by the server of the content provider"); spam messages. **Prior consent (Sec. 3.1):** messages "shall be sent only to subscribers who have prior consent or have specifically opted-in", with a method to opt out. **Time (3.2):** not sent "between 9:00 PM to 7:00 AM except on paid subscription services". **Charges (3.3):** recipients not charged unless they opted in. **Non-response (3.4):** subscribers who do not reply are considered opted out. **Sender identification (3.5):** messages "shall display the name of the PTE"; content providers' messages show their company names. **Opt-out details (3.6):** "valid addresses or numbers" for cease requests and an opt-out command. | What is established | Baseline rules for commercial/promotional broadcasts and surveys sent by PTEs and content providers. |

## Registration path — what the texts read show, and what they do not

This is a map of the texts, **not a classification of our business**:

1. **2008 VAS regime (LR-03):** messaging services including SMS are VAS; a Certificate of Registration is
   required; equipment limited to processing, routing, and storing messages; transmission leased from PTEs.
2. **RA 12234 and its IRR (LR-02, LR-05):** VAS providers are DTIPs **only to the extent** of data
   transmission; DTIPs register (last mile, middle mile) or are authorized (gateway, core/backbone); prior
   NTC registration and authorization rules are superseded (scope unclear); VAS registrations are not
   mentioned in the IRR's transitional provisions.
3. **Reported 2025–2026 NTC issuances (LR-04, LR-16, LR-17):** reportedly change VAS registration and set
   DTIP eligibility, including a "modified VAS CoR" for VAS that is not data transmission — **unverified**.

Whether our proposed A2P SMS aggregation is VAS, a DTIP, both, or neither — and which certificate applies —
**requires a written opinion from qualified Philippine telecommunications counsel** (D-120).
